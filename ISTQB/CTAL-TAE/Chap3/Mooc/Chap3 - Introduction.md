"In a previous section, we explored how to prepare for test automation by understanding infrastructure

In this section, we'll explore the architectural concepts that form the backbone of any successful

test automation implementation.

We'll also explore different approaches for automating test cases, from simple capture playback and

linear scripting to more sophisticated techniques like structured scripting, data driven testing,

and behavior driven development.

Finally, we'll look at design principles and patterns that can elevate your automation code from merely

functional to truly professional.
